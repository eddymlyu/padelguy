Field notes

Built for
small brands

Most factories are set up for big orders. If you run a 1 to 3 person brand, these are the problems that cost you time and money.

#1

MOQs that don't fit

Factories open to realistic first runs, and a path to scale.

#2

Sample ≠ bulk

Golden samples, written specs and pre-shipment checks so batch 3 matches batch 1.

#3

Hidden landed cost

Unit price, packaging, freight and duties broken down before you commit.

#4

No backup supplier

A second qualified source so a holiday shutdown or bad batch doesn't stop sales.

#5

Packaging

Custom boxes, tubes, labels and inserts, sourced and checked with the product.

#6

EU compliance

Material documents and labelling sorted early, not after the goods arrive.
